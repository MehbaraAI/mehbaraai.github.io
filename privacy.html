Privacy

Short version: this website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and does not collect personal data itself.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you open it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Applying to present

The application form is a Google Form, shown on the page or opened in a new tab. What you submit is processed by Google and used by MEHBARA only to plan sessions and to contact you about your application. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

Luma

Session registration and the sessions calendar run on Luma. If you register for a session or follow the calendar, your details are handled by Luma under its own privacy policy and shared with MEHBARA as the organiser. You can unfollow or cancel at any time.

Stored in your browser

Your language choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data held about you. Write to mehbaraai@gmail.com.

الخطوط

تُحمَّل خطوط الموقع من Google Fonts، لذا يرسل متصفحك عنوان IP الخاص بك إلى Google عند تحميل الصفحة. للمزيد راجع سياسة الخصوصية لدى Google.

التقدّم لعرض ورقة

استمارة التقديم هي استمارة Google، تظهر داخل الصفحة أو تُفتح في نافذة جديدة. تعالج Google ما ترسله، ولا تستخدمه محبرة إلا لتنظيم الجلسات والتواصل معك بشأن طلبك. ويمكنك طلب حذفه في أي وقت.
