Vision

An open and collaborative Arab AI research ecosystem in which researchers and students learn, collaborate, conduct rigorous research, and contribute to global scientific knowledge.

The path we want every member to be able to take

  1. Student
  2. Learner
  3. Contributor
  4. Researcher
  5. Mentor

Mission

From knowledge to research

  1. Connect Arab AI researchers, engineers and students across institutions and countries.
  2. Make high-quality AI research more accessible through open reading and discussion.
  3. Turn discussions into concrete research questions, and questions into collaborations.
  4. Promote open research and reproducibility, and support early-career researchers.

What it is

A reading group that takes papers seriously

MEHBARA brings together researchers, engineers and students from across the Arab world and the diaspora to read AI research together. Sessions are online, 45–75 minutes, in Arabic or English, and open to anyone who wants to think carefully about the work.

The name means inkwell (⁨محبرة⁩): the vessel from which knowledge flows to the page.

  1. The paperThe presenter walks through the problem, method, experiments and results.
  2. The critiqueAssumptions, evaluation, limitations and reproducibility, discussed openly.
  3. Open questionsEvery question the paper leaves open is written down and shared.
  4. What nextIs there a question here worth investigating together?

Themes

What we read

Any strong AI paper is welcome. These are the areas we expect to read most, with a particular interest in work that matters for Arabic and the region.
